    07 — The Agarthan code

    Strength, kept in peace

    The builders below the ice never made arms for conquest. They made them to protect what they loved, and they kept them with discipline. Every customer who passes our gate carries the same code.

    1. ITreat every firearm as loaded. Every time, even right after you checked.
    2. IIKeep the muzzle pointed somewhere safe. Never at anything you aren’t willing to destroy.
    3. IIIFinger off the trigger until your sights are on target and you’ve decided to fire.
    4. IVKnow your target and what’s beyond it.

    Lock it up at home · train often · keep the peace
