FFL transfer services

The gate in the wall

Bought a firearm online, at auction or from a private seller? Ship it to our FFL. We receive it, log it, run the background check and hand it over, by the book.

Questions
01 — How it works

Five steps through the gate

  1. 01

    Buy it anywhere

    Any online retailer, auction site or private seller.

  2. 02

    Ship to our FFL

    The seller needs a copy of our license. Ask and we’ll send it to them directly.

  3. 03

    We log it

    It’s entered in our bound book on arrival, and we call you.

  4. 04

    Clear the checkpoint

    Valid photo ID, ATF Form 4473 and a NICS background check.

  5. 05

    Take it home

    Approved and complete. Welcome back through the gate.

What to bring

  • A valid government-issued photo ID with your current address (driver’s license or state ID).
  • If your ID address is out of date, a second government document showing your current address (vehicle registration, voter card, property tax bill).
  • Your state’s permit or license, if your state requires one to buy (for example, a handgun purchase permit).
  • The seller’s name and order number, so we can match the shipment.

Before you buy

  • Make sure the firearm is legal to own where you live. We can’t transfer an item your state prohibits.
  • Call or email us first so we know it’s coming.
  • Handguns and receivers: 21+. Long guns: 18+. Federal law sets the floor; your state may set more.
  • We inspect each arrival. Report any damage to the seller before the transfer completes.

04 — Questions

Transfer FAQ

How long does a transfer take?

Once the firearm arrives and is logged, the transfer itself usually takes 20–30 minutes in person. Most NICS checks come back in minutes. Some come back “delayed”; we’ll keep you posted.

What happens if my background check is delayed or denied?

A delay means NICS needs more time to research, and we follow federal and state rules on how long to wait before proceeding. If a transfer is denied, we can’t release the firearm; we’ll explain your options, including returning it to the seller (shipping at your cost) and appealing the decision.

Can I ship a firearm to you from another state?

Yes. Licensed dealers ship firearms to other licensed dealers across state lines. Handguns must transfer to residents of the dealer’s state, and long guns can transfer to residents of other states only where both states’ laws allow it. Ask before you buy.

Do you accept ammunition, parts or accessories?

Under federal law, ammunition and most parts don’t require an FFL, though some states regulate ammunition sales and some sellers will only ship to a dealer. Ask us first; there may be a receiving fee.

Do you handle NFA items?

Suppressors, short-barreled rifles and other NFA items require a dealer with Special Occupational Tax status. Call us and we’ll tell you exactly what we can do.

How long will you hold my firearm?

Please pick up within a reasonable time once we call. Long-term storage may carry a fee. Ask us if you need longer.
